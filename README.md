# 🌸 Evergarden

This is my digital notebook, but dressed like a garden 🌿  
I use it to keep my trading journals, jot down scraps of thoughts,  
and save pieces of inspiration that feel meaningful.

---

## Map of the Garden

- **meadows/** — trading only (daily logs, weekly reviews, monthly blooms)  
- **reflections/** — personal journaling and life check-ins  
- **scraps/** — unfiltered notes, random thoughts, raw emotion  
- **moodboards/** — ongoing collections of quotes, vibes, colors, and doodles  
- **petals/** — small scripts and tools to help everything run  
- **reference/** — CAD notes, trading playbook, cheatsheets  
- **life/** — fitness notes, trip planning, budget logs  
- **creative/** — keyboards, fiction, experiments  

---

## 🌼 Garden Notes

Some months will be full of writing, others will be quiet.  
That’s how gardens work — seasons come and go.  

I don’t expect to write every day.  
Sometimes it’s a bloom, sometimes just a seed.  
The point is to give myself a cozy place to plant ideas and watch them grow.  

> *This garden grows at its own pace.*
